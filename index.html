Studio Pitch & Design Specs

Cascade Harmony: Zen Flow • Commercial Overview
1. The Market Innovation (100% Unique) Existing vertical drop games (Drop 7, 2048 Drop) trap pieces in isolated silos. Cascade Harmony removes the walls: stones physically leap sideways through the air between columns, pulling the floor from under upper stones to spark automatic domino cascades!
2. The 3-in-1 Genetic Hybrid Matrix
  • Block Blast: 3-piece hand tray, cascading domino combos, and full horizontal row clears.
  • Screw Jam: Vertical gravity column drop physics, tactile weight, and tactical blockers/boosters.
  • Hexa Sort: Airborne magnetic stack jumping with tactile ASMR feedback.
3. Target Audience: Adults 30+ High-stress working adults and commuters seeking calming yet high-dopamine mental relaxation. No stress timers, single-thumb drag-or-tap play, water ripples, and procedural Tibetan singing bowl chords (pentatonic C4-C5 scale).
4. Retention & Commercial Monetization
  • Skill Boosters: Zen Hammer (single block smash) & Magnet Vortex (board pull) earned via 3x combos or rewarded ads.
  • Live-Ops: Koi Pond Garden customizer & Daily Zen Domino Challenges.
  • High-Conversion Ad Placements: Rewarded extra moves & cosmetic stone themes.
