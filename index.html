Cascade Harmony — Studio Specs

1. The 3-Game Breeding Formula
  • Block Blast Hand Mechanic: 3 pre-spawned multi-stone pieces forced to fit into finite slots.
  • Screw Jam Gravity Pins: Vertical tubes with top-layer locks, domino tumble cascades, and goal boxes.
  • Hexa Sort Magnetic Flight: High-satisfaction tile leaps between adjacent stacks merging into 4-pops.
2. Target Audience & Commercial Viability

Ages 30+ adults seeking high-tactility, stress-relief gaming with tactical depth. Replaces stressful timers with relaxing pentatonic chime feedback.

3. Why it stands out on the App Store

Solves the dead-end puzzle trap: instead of losing when a column fills up, placing a matching stone near it pulls stones out to trigger a multi-column blossom clear!
