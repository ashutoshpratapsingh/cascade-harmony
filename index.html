Meow Ripple: Game Design Specs

100% Unique • Zero Clones • Casual Appeal

🌊 1. Truly Unique: Water-Ripple Impulse Physics

Unlike every generic puzzle on Google Play, Meow Ripple contains NO vertical bottle tubes, NO match-3 grids, and NO sliding blocks.

⭐ The Breakthrough: The player interacts with natural water surface tension! Tapping the pond creates concentric ripple waves that physically push and collide floating, living cat puffs.

🐾 2. Instant Comprehension & Cute Kawaii Animals
  • • 2-Second Learning Curve: A 4-year-old or 70-year-old immediately understands that water ripples push things. No manual or reading required.
  • • Living Characters: Chubby kittens blink, squish, breath, and purr. Colliding matching cats triggers a joyful cuddle-merge with ASMR purrs.
  • • Zero Stress: No punishing countdown clocks, pure sensory delight.
🎁 3. Rewarded Boosters & Live-Ops Hooks
  • • 🧶 Yarn Herd Booster: Throws a bouncy yarn ball that pulls all kittens toward it in one swoop.
  • • 🐟 Tuna Snack Booster: Drops a delicious fish snack that attracts all cats across the pond into mega-merges.
  • • Collectible Cat Outfits: Rewarded videos unlock cute hats, flower crowns, and sleeping caps for your kittens!
