# CASCADE HARMONY (Pro Edition)

> **Concept:** The genetic cross-breeding of 2024's 3 biggest mobile sensations: *Screw Jam* (gravity drops), *Hexa Sort* (magnetic tile leaping), and *Block Blast* (3-piece selection & musical combos).

---

## 🎮 How to Play the Prototype

1. **Launch the Game:**
   * Open [`index.html`](file:///e:/THE%20DARK/index.html) directly in any browser on your phone or PC.
2. **Step 1: Choose a Piece:**
   * Look at the 3 stone stacks in the bottom tray. Tap one to select it. (Watch for the rare **Rainbow Prism Wildcard**!).
3. **Step 2: Drop into a Column:**
   * Tap any of the 6 columns in the water pond to drop your stones.
   * Watch the stones fall with real gravity (*thock!*).
   * Notice the glowing **✨ MATCH!** beacons showing you where matching colors are waiting!
4. **Step 3: Watch the Magnetic Cascade!**
   * If neighboring columns share the same color, **the stones will magnetically fly through the air** to merge together!
5. **Step 4: Harmony Blasts & Combos:**
   * When a stack reaches 4 matching stones, or when a full horizontal row fills across the pond:
   * **BLAST!** The stones dissolve into glowing lotus petals.
   * The stones above drop down, triggering **secondary leaps and domino chain reactions!**
6. **Zen Boosters:**
   * Use **🔨 ZEN HAMMER** to smash any single blocking stone.
   * Use **🧲 MAGNET PULL** to suck all matching colors across the board together!
7. **Level Up:**
   * Fill your Lotus Target bar to trigger the **🌟 LEVEL COMPLETE! 🌟** celebration and advance to the next level!
